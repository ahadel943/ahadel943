### 1. Repeat Purchase Rate Analysis
**SQL | PostgreSQL**

Analyzed customer retention and repeat purchasing behavior by evaluating repeat purchase rate, cancellation impact, pricing, geographic, and category-level trends.

**Key Finding:** Repeat customers generated **78% of total revenue**, while order cancellations were the strongest indicator of lower repeat purchasing.

### 2. E-commerce Conversion Funnel Analysis
**SQL | PostgreSQL**

Analyzed a two-year e-commerce conversion funnel using session-level event data to identify conversion rates and drop-offs across funnel stages.

**Key Findings:** Overall conversion rate was **13.86%**. Mobile sessions converted at **8.80%** compared with **23.26%** on Desktop, while the Luxury category recorded the lowest conversion rate at **3.22%**.

🔗 [View Project](https://github.com/ahadel943/e-commerce_funnel_conversion_analysis)

### 3. E-commerce Cart Abandonment Analysis
**SQL | PostgreSQL**

Analyzed **361K+ e-commerce shopping carts** to identify abandonment patterns and conversion bottlenecks across the purchase journey.

**Key Findings:** Overall Cart Abandonment Rate was **82.06%**, with **222K+ abandoned carts** in the $1,500+ value segment, while nearly **40% of high-value carts never reached checkout**.
